Decision-rule inventory for the static-batch 8x8 path (2026-10-24)
================================================================

Scope: effective E177/E179 development evaluation path, and E180 replacement.
This is a source audit, not a claim that the frozen checkpoint performs well.
The test panel remains sealed.

Problem definition
------------------
- All N jobs are available at time zero; N is determined by jobs/capacity,
  not initial-yard fill (E164.run.static_case). Kept.
- The environment's inbound view exposes the first waiting job; later jobs
  cannot skip it. E164.objective.FixedAdmissionStorageEnv.step checks B1..BN
  admission order. Kept.
- Legal Accept, Deliver, Reconfigure and event-Defer macros are enumerated
  from physical initiation and paths (E83.legal.physical_frontier). Kept.
- Defer advances to the next observable delivery-window opening; it is not
  available without a strictly future event (E152.policy.EventDeferRule and
  E152.option, with E164 stored-window override). Kept. It is not forbidden
  just because an inbound block is waiting.
- Due date is first-storage time plus storage duration; handoff window is
  absolute +/-20 primitive steps (E164.objective, E168.objective). Kept.
- E180 J is 2T + E + 0.5W + 0.1L + 10R. W accrues per released, unadmitted
  job-step; all jobs are released at zero. E180.objective. E164's zero W is
  removed. This objective applies to all future comparison arms.
- The finite episode horizon is an execution-error cap, not an action
  preference (E164/E168 horizon patch, E159.run.episode). Kept at 2600 for
  this pilot; hitting it is reported as failure.

Certification
-------------
- The current actual boundary must have a SAFE joint certificate before
  action construction (E162.frontier._current_certificate, invoked by
  E176.guard.build). Kept.
- Candidate successors must be joint-SAFE before execution; UNKNOWN is
  fail-closed under the fixed proof budget (E159.run._candidate,
  E162.run._certified_choice, E158.selector). Kept.
- Joint proof includes outflow completion and pickup access for continuing
  admission (E122.flow). Kept.
- E172 strengthens the declared admission-capacity invariant: every free,
  unreserved storage cell remains pickup-reachable, not merely one. Kept as
  the operational feasibility requirement. This may be conservative relative
  to weaker access definitions and must be stated as such in the manuscript.
- E122 also requires the transporter, at its successor position, to be able
  to approach pickup. E175's recorded physically legal Delivers fail this
  *native* return-access condition at three exits with inbound jobs remaining.
  This is not E172's all-free-cell comparison and must not be waived without
  changing the transporter-return model.
- Proof caching and lazy certification of proposals/targets (E158, E159,
  E161) may alter query order, not the selected SAFE frontier action. Kept
  subject to their parity tests. An UNKNOWN remains excluded.

Ranking
-------
- The five-head residual critic combines nominal immediate components with
  learned future components; E180 restores nonzero nominal admission wait
  and uses the existing 0.5 wait head (E147.model, E180.objective). Kept.
- The grouped selector scores SAFE temporal modes by normalized log-mean-exp,
  then chooses the highest-Q candidate in the winning mode, with stable key
  tie-breaking (E158.selector, viability_graph_preference_conditioned).
  This is a ranking preference, not a feasibility rule. Kept unchanged.
- During frozen evaluation, no epsilon/softmax exploration occurs. E156's
  scale-free softmax is training-only. Kept out of this pilot.
- Proof-dependent successor feature masking in E158 is a representation
  choice applied symmetrically in the compared arms, not an action filter.
  Kept unchanged.

Progress guard
--------------
- Event observations reset the Reconfigure streak (E153 guard state as used
  by E176.guard). Kept.
- At the bound, E176.choice checks the non-Reconfigure candidates first and
  masks Reconfigure only if at least one is joint-SAFE. Kept.
- If none is SAFE, it takes exactly one currently certified completion-witness
  step, then rechecks at the next boundary (E176.choice). Kept. It does not
  commit to the entire emptying witness or choose Accept over Deliver.
- The retained witness is a liveness fallback, not a cost-optimal ranking.
  Its use is counted separately.

Removed / inactive
------------------
- E164.frontier and E168.frontier: when any SAFE Accept exists, replace the
  entire frontier with Accepts. REMOVE; it expresses admission preference
  as a constraint and can suppress overdue deliveries.
- E168.frontier: when inbound remains and Accept is blocked, remove Defer.
  REMOVE; it expresses urgency preference as a constraint.
- E171.frontier: nested intake-restoration lookahead. Already absent from
  E177/E179 and not imported into E180. Leave out; E172 access prevention
  is the declared capacity invariant.
- E153 and E162's earlier selector/frontier liveness patches are superseded
  for actual E180 choices by direct E176.guard.build and E176.choice.choose.
  The E153 guard object still stores the event counter; its legacy forcing
  method does not select E180 actions.
- E179's lazy-Accept speed patch is not activated. Its parity result is valid
  for the old Accept-first protocol only, and it must be rechecked under E180
  before adoption.
